# Vendor conversation guide (in person, 15 to 20 minutes)

Goal of the first meeting: learn, not sell. Fill one row of data/real/vendor_sheet_template.csv per vendor.

1. Introduce yourself in one line: "I'm building Ocazionally, a platform that brings event vendors and customers together, starting with society and festival events in Gandhinagar and Ahmedabad."
2. Ask:
   - What events do you do most, and what is your typical price for a small society event, a wedding, a festival?
   - Which months are busiest and which are empty?
   - How many events can you handle in a month?
   - How do customers find you today?
   - What problems do you face (last-minute cancellations, late payments, price bargaining)?
   - Would you pay for leads, or prefer a commission per booking?
3. Close: ask if they'd be open to a small pilot event with Ocazionally and get a phone number.
4. Right after the meeting, fill the sheet while you remember it.
